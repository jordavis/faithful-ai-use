# Faithful AI Use

A free skill for Claude that helps you use AI without letting it stand in for God, your own growth, real relationships, or real life.

It's based on Elder Gerrit W. Gong's message [*Faith, Dignity, and Human Flourishing: Hearing God's Voice in an Age of Artificial Intelligence*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ts5Z64A0Vv4) and its four relationships: God (Thou), self (I), others (They), and the natural world (It).

> "Let AI inform, but you decide with the Lord." — Elder Gerrit W. Gong

## What it does

- **Everyday work:** it just helps. No lectures.
- **Big decisions** (a job, marriage, a move): it lays out the factors, then hands the choice back to you.
- **Talks, lessons, and homework:** it tutors and reviews your draft. It won't write them for you.
- **Heartfelt messages:** it asks questions until you find your own words.
- **Venting or loneliness:** it listens, then points you to people who know and love you.

It pushes back once, offers the right kind of help, then respects your call. Type **"gut check"** before any question to have it check the question first.

## Install it in Claude

Works on Free, Pro, and Max plans, on the web and desktop app.

1. **Download** [`faithful-ai-use.zip`](faithful-ai-use.zip) from this repo. Click the file, then the download button. Don't unzip it.
2. **Turn on code execution.** In Claude, go to **Settings › Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**. Skills need it.
3. **Upload the skill.** Go to **Customize › Skills**, click **+**, then **+ Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and choose the zip.
4. **Make sure it's on.** It should appear in your skills list with its toggle on.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, your organization owner has to allow skills first. If you don't see the upload option, ask them.

## Try it

You don't need to call it. Claude uses it when a request fits. Try:

- "Just tell me which job to take."
- "Write my sacrament meeting talk."
- "Write a heartfelt letter to my wife."
- "gut check: what's the best way to study the scriptures?"

To force it on, name it: "Use my faithful-ai-use skill and help me with…"

## Make it yours

The skill is plain text. Unzip it, open `SKILL.md`, and edit it to fit your own boundaries, then zip the folder again and re-upload. Keep the folder named `faithful-ai-use`, or change the `name:` line at the top of `SKILL.md` to match a new folder name.

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md`: the instructions Claude follows
- `references/message-summary.md`: a paraphrased summary of the message with brief quotes, used for grounding. Watch the [full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ts5Z64A0Vv4) for the real thing.
